# Animeverse Studios

An independent AI-powered anime and comic storytelling studio.

## Mission
Create original fictional animated stories for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

## First Series
**Working title:** The Last City

**Genre:** Dark fantasy, mystery, science fiction.

**Core premise:** In a world where memories are used as currency, a young man discovers that the government has erased part of his past.

## Episode Format
- 45–60 seconds per episode
- Vertical 9:16 video
- 6–8 visual scenes
- Anime/comic visual style
- Narration, sound effects and subtitles
- Strong cliffhanger at the end

## Production Goals
1. Develop original characters and locations.
2. Maintain consistent character designs and story continuity.
3. Generate scripts and visual assets.
4. Assemble finished short videos.
5. Publish to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
6. Track views, retention and audience feedback.

## Technology Principles
- Android-first workflow.
- Prefer free and open-source tools.
- Use cloud services where local computing is unavailable.
- Never expose passwords, API keys or access tokens.
- Keep production modular so services can be replaced.

## First Milestone
Produce one complete 60-second pilot episode.
